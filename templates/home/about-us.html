About Us

About Us

At PG Home Dynamics, we are committed to helping homeowners create more energy-efficient, comfortable, and healthier living spaces. Our comprehensive Home Energy Audit service assesses your home’s energy use, identifying areas where you can reduce waste, lower utility bills, and improve efficiency. From insulation and air sealing to HVAC systems and appliance performance, our experts provide actionable recommendations to optimize your home’s energy performance.

In addition, our Healthy Home Evaluation goes beyond energy efficiency to address factors affecting indoor air quality, moisture control, and overall environmental health. We assess potential hazards such as mold, allergens, carbon monoxide, and ventilation issues offering practical solutions to enhance your home’s air quality and occupant well-being.

Our Commitment

Our commitment is to empower homeowners by providing comprehensive energy audits and Healthy Home Evaluations that optimize comfort, reduce energy consumption, and promote healthier living environments.

Our Values
