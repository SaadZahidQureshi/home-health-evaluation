Building

Ensure gutters are working and downspouts direct water away from the foundation

  • Ensure gutters are working and downspouts direct water away from the foundation
  • Grade dirt away from the house. It should be lower away from the foundation than it is next to it.
  • Repair leaks (roof, doors, windows, plumbing).
  • Repair foundation cracks.
  • Install storm windows to help prevent condensation.
  • Control moisture in a crawl space with a properly-installed ground cover.
  • Be wary of passive ventilation – a method of introducing outside air into the home without fans – in a basement or crawl space. This may bring humid outdoor air into an already damp environment.

Ensure bathrooms have fans vented to the outside.
